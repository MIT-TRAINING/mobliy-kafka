Confluent Platform overview & architecture

Confluent Platform is still Kafka at the centre: the same brokers, KRaft controllers, topics and partitions you ran in Modules 1–5. Confluent adds services around it. Click each box to see its job, port and licence. Then press Send a CDR to follow one voice record through the platform. Switch the edition to see which parts remain without an Enterprise licence, and stop Schema Registry to see what fails.

Admin takeaway: everything you learnt on Apache Kafka still applies. server.properties, min.insync.replicas, ISR, reassignment and rolling restarts all work the same way on Confluent Server. What is new is a set of extra services, each with its own port, logs, heap and upgrade schedule. Add them to your monitoring and your rolling-restart runbook.
Production trap: Schema Registry, ksqlDB and Control Center keep their state in Kafka topics (_schemas, _confluent-ksql-*, _confluent-*). If someone deletes _schemas or sets a short retention on it, every schema ID disappears, and consumers can no longer deserialize records. Leave internal topics alone, and give them replication factor 3.
