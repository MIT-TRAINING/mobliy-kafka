Confluent RBAC (Role-Based Access Control)

With RBAC you don't list Kafka operations one by one. You give a principal a role at a scope: an organization, environment, cluster, or a single topic or consumer group. Your task: the billing-svc app runs as service account sa-l07-app and must consume l07.cdr.voice with group l07.billing, and do nothing else. Add role bindings, run the tests, and try for least privilege.

Run as sa-l07-app:

Admin gotcha: a consumer needs two bindings: DeveloperRead on the topic and on its consumer group. With only the topic binding, the app fails with GROUP_AUTHORIZATION_FAILED. This is the most common failure on a new RBAC setup.
Production trap: RBAC only adds permissions. There is no deny role. If you gave EnvironmentAdmin to an app, you can't take one topic away from it. Delete the broad binding and grant narrow ones: confluent iam rbac role-binding delete ….
Same model on Platform: Confluent Platform uses the same resource roles (DeveloperRead … ResourceOwner, Operator) through MDS. Its admin roles are SystemAdmin, ClusterAdmin, UserAdmin, SecurityAdmin and AuditAdmin (Topic 3). On the shared Platform cluster you hold ResourceOwner on Topic:l07. and Group:l07. as prefixes.
