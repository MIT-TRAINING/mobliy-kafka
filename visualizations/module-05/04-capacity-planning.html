Topic 4

Cluster capacity planning

How many brokers does an operator with 20 million subscribers need for its usage records? Disk, network in, network out and the replication factor each set a minimum number of brokers, and the largest one decides. Move the sliders to see which limit binds. Raise retention, add consumer groups, switch compression off, and watch the binding limit change.

The arithmetic

LimitFormulaNotes
Ingresssubscribers × records/day ÷ 86 400 × bytes ÷ compressionSize disk with the average rate, and network and partitions with the peak. For telecom, the evening peak or a big event (a New Year's Eve CDR surge) sets the peak.
Diskavg ingress × retention × RF ÷ (disk per broker × max utilisation)Leave headroom for a reassignment, during which old and new copies exist at the same time (Topic 1), and for segments waiting to be deleted
Network inpeak × RF ÷ (network per broker × max utilisation)Producer traffic plus copies arriving from leaders
Network outpeak × (RF − 1 + consumer groups) ÷ (network per broker × max utilisation)Every consumer group reads the whole stream. Usually the first network limit you hit.
Partitionsmax(peak ÷ throughput per partition, consumers in the largest group)Measure throughput per partition with kafka-producer-perf-test.sh and kafka-consumer-perf-test.sh on your own hardware
Traditional MQQueue depth × message sizeKafka stores everything for the whole retention period, even after it has been consumed, so retention drives disk, not consumer lag
Admin gotcha: cdr.voice is keyed by MSISDN so that each subscriber's records stay in order. Adding partitions later sends many keys to a different partition, so ordering breaks for in-flight data. Size keyed topics for 2–3 years of growth on day one (kafka-topics.sh --create --partitions 24 …). Don't plan on running --alter --partitions later.
Admin takeaway: plan to run with a broker down. When one fails, the rest serve its leaders, and its replacement copies a full broker's data. That's why the plan keeps a 60% ceiling and an N+1 broker. Repeat the exercise every quarter using measured rates, from kafka-log-dirs.sh --describe for disk and broker BytesInPerSec / BytesOutPerSec for network (Module 8).
