ISR, leader election & handling broker failures

Partition cdr.voice-0 on your 3-broker Docker cluster has replicas 1, 2 and 3. Only replicas in the ISR (in-sync replicas) can take over cleanly when the leader fails. Click a broker to crash or restart it. Kill the leader, then kill a second broker, then all three. Bring the wrong one back first and see whether the partition comes back. Switch to acks=1 to find acknowledged CDRs that later vanish.

The moving parts

LEO
Log-end offset: the offset of the next record this replica will write. Each replica has its own LEO.
high watermark
The highest offset that every ISR member has copied. Consumers only read below it, and acks=all producers get their ack when it passes their record.
ISR
The leader plus the followers that are caught up. A follower that falls behind for longer than replica.lag.time.max.ms (default 30 s) is removed.
fencing
If a broker misses heartbeats for broker.session.timeout.ms (default 9 s), the KRaft controller fences it. It is removed from every ISR, and any partition it led gets a new leader.
leader epoch
Goes up by one with every new leader. A returning replica uses it to find where its log diverges, and deletes everything after that point.

Admin takeaway: look for trouble before it becomes an outage. kafka-topics.sh --bootstrap-server $BS --describe --under-replicated-partitions lists partitions that are missing a replica. --under-min-isr-partitions lists partitions that are refusing acks=all writes. --unavailable-partitions lists partitions with no leader. Any output from the last two means an incident is already under way.
Production trap: an unclean election is a deliberate choice to lose data, not a recovery procedure. Leave unclean.leader.election.enable=false. If you must bring a partition back, do it for that one partition with kafka-leader-election.sh --election-type unclean --topic cdr.voice --partition 0, after the business has accepted the loss. Kafka 4.1+ also tracks eligible leader replicas (the Elr column in --describe): replicas that left the ISR but still hold every committed record. This simulation leaves ELR out and shows the classic ISR rules.
