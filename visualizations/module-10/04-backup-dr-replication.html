Backup and DR: Confluent Replicator and multi-datacenter replication

Kafka has no "backup to tape" for live data. Disaster recovery means a second cluster that holds a copy of your topics and enough of your consumers' positions to carry on. Cross-site copies are asynchronous, so a site failure costs you whatever hadn't been copied yet (RPO), and consumer positions are copied only periodically, so some CDRs get billed twice. Start the traffic, pick a tool, then fail Site A and fail over. Try a bigger replication lag, a longer offset-sync interval, and Replicator without its interceptor.

When RPO must be zero: a stretched cluster

    
Every tool above copies asynchronously, so RPO is at least the replication lag.
      A Multi-Region Cluster (Confluent Platform) is one cluster spread across sites. Synchronous replicas in both
      sites, acks=all and a matching min.insync.replicas give RPO 0. The cost is cross-site
      latency on every write, plus a third location for the KRaft controller quorum. Observers (asynchronous replicas)
      add a cheaper copy without slowing writes.

  

  
Admin takeaway: after any failover, the consumers will reprocess some CDRs. Make billing
    idempotent: deduplicate on cdrId, which is the same at both sites. Don't use topic-partition-offset
    keys, because offsets differ on Replicator and MirrorMaker 2 copies. Then rehearse the failover and time it: the RTO
    is your runbook, not the tool.

  
Production trap: Replicator's and MirrorMaker 2's destination topics are ordinary,
    writable topics. A producer pointed at Site B by mistake writes records that Site A never gets back. Restrict who
    can write to them with RBAC, or use Cluster Linking, whose mirror topics are read-only.
