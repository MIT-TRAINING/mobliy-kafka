Topic 5

Migration: Apache Kafka → Confluent Kafka

You must move the CDR pipeline from the Apache cluster (apache-kafka.lab.internal) to Confluent Platform (cp-kafka.lab.internal) without stopping billing for long, losing CDRs or billing any twice. Pick a strategy and decide where the new consumer group starts. Tick what each client must change, then step through the runbook. Aim for the capstone targets: billing paused for 5 minutes or less, 0 CDRs lost, every duplicate explained, and a rollback within 15 minutes.

Admin takeaway: decide where the new group starts, and prove it before you cut over. earliest re-bills the whole history. latest silently skips the CDRs written during the pause. A translated position, or kafka-consumer-groups.sh --reset-offsets --to-datetime with a safety margin, gives a few explained duplicates. Then verify with CDR IDs, never offsets: offsets are different on the new cluster.
Production trap: the Replicator connector's configuration holds your Apache credentials, and anyone who can read that connector can see them. Use a dedicated replication user with read-only rights, keep the file out of Git, and delete the connector when the migration ends.
