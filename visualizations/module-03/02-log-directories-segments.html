Log directories & segment management

Everything lives under log.dirs, one directory per partition, and each partition's log is split into segments. Produce records below and watch segments roll — then click a segment to inspect its files. Only the active segment accepts appends, and only sealed segments can ever be deleted or compacted.

Why the active segment matters: retention and compaction never touch the active segment. "retention.ms is 1 day but I still see month-old data" is almost always a low-traffic topic whose active segment has not rolled. Lower segment.ms (time) or segment.bytes (size) on such topics if expiry precision matters — at the cost of more, smaller files.

JBOD: multiple log directories per broker

log.dirs=/data/d1,/data/d2 — Kafka distributes replicas across the disks and uses their combined capacity. Fail a disk and see exactly what goes offline.

JBOD behaviour to remember: new partitions go to the directory with the fewest partitions; adding a disk does not move existing data (Kafka has no built-in partition-to-disk rebalancing); and Confluent's Tiered Storage does not support JBOD — it requires a single log directory.

On an unclean shutdown the broker replays the tail of every log, rebuilding indexes and truncating uncommitted data using leader-epoch-checkpoint. Raise num.recovery.threads.per.data.dir (default 1) on brokers with many partitions to shorten restarts — one of the simplest availability wins in Kafka.
