Message retention & log compaction

Kafka never deletes a record because someone read it. Data leaves a partition only through its cleanup.policy: delete (drop whole old segments by time or size) or compact (keep the latest value per key).

1 · cleanup.policy=delete — time- and size-based retention

Retention works on whole segments, not individual records. A segment (.log + .index + .timeindex files) is eligible once its newest record is older than retention.ms, or when the partition exceeds retention.bytes. The active segment rolls when it hits segment.bytes (default 1 GB) or segment.ms (default 7 days) — here each segment holds 6 records to keep things visible. Default retention.ms is 7 days; retention.bytes defaults to −1 (unlimited) and applies per partition.

What compaction guarantees

  • At least the latest record for every key is retained.
  • Offsets never change — compaction leaves gaps (notice the missing numbers).
  • Order of the surviving records is preserved.
  • A consumer reading from offset 0 sees the full current state.

Tombstones

A record with a key and a null value marks the key for deletion. It survives compaction for delete.retention.ms (default 24 h) so consumers can see the delete, then it's removed too. In this demo a tombstone is removed on the second cleaner run.
