Root causes of message loss: acks, replication factor, quotas and throttling

Kafka doesn't lose messages at random. Loss means one of two things: the cluster acknowledged a CDR that later disappeared, or the producer gave up on one and nobody looked at the error. Pick a failure, set the producer and topic, then press Send 24 CDRs. Try to get a red "acknowledged then lost" cell, then change one setting at a time until you can't.

Quotas and throttlingThe broker delays responses. Batches wait in the producer until delivery.timeout.ms, then fail, or send() blocks for max.block.ms when buffer.memory is full.Handle the send callback; size quotas and Cloud capacity with headroom; alert on throttlingLab: producer_byte_rate client quotas. Cloud: cluster-type limits (client_limit_milliseconds)
Admin takeaway: acks=all means "every replica currently in the ISR has it". It is only as strong as min.insync.replicas: with 1, an ISR of just the leader still acknowledges. Use RF 3 with min.insync.replicas=2, so every acknowledged CDR is on at least two brokers in two zones.
Production trap: throttling never deletes data on the broker. The loss happens in your application, when a send() callback receives a TimeoutException and nobody checks it. Grep the code for producer.send(record) with no callback and no .get().
