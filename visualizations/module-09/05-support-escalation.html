The Confluent support and escalation model

A support subscription doesn't fix your role bindings, and Confluent can't see your application logs. Good escalation means sending each problem to the team that owns it, at the right severity, with the evidence that team needs in the first message. Pick an incident, choose the owner, the severity and the evidence, then press Escalate. See whether the ticket lands or comes back with questions.

Admin takeaway: the exact severity wording and the response targets come from your Confluent support plan or subscription, so read yours before you need it. Tickets go through the Confluent support portal (support.confluent.io). Every ticket needs the cluster ID, a UTC time window and the exact error text. Without them, the first reply is a question.
Production trap: never paste an API secret, an LDAP password or a full cp.properties into a ticket. Support doesn't need it to diagnose anything. Remove secrets from configs and logs, and if one slips out, rotate it (confluent api-key delete and create a new one, or change the LDAP password).
