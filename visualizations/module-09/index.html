Troubleshooting & Preventing Message Loss on Confluent Kafka

Interactive visualizations for each Module 9 topic. Each page has a hands-on simulation you can play with, followed by a 5-question self-quiz with explanations.

Objective: Troubleshoot Confluent Kafka clusters and diagnose, resolve, and prevent message loss and other production issues.

Common errors & log analysis

Pick a symptom from the CDR pipeline, read the client log, the broker log and the metrics side by side, and find the stage of the request where it failed and whether retrying can help.
