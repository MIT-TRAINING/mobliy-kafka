Topic 5

Error handling and retry patterns

Kafka won't take a record away from a consumer that keeps failing on it. There's no redelivery count and no automatic dead-letter queue, as there is in MQ. You choose what happens. Feed the consumer transient failures (the rating service times out twice, then works) and poison pills (malformed CDRs that never work), and compare four strategies on lag, loss and ordering.

Strategy
normal CDR transient failure (fails 2×, then works) poison pill (always fails)

Consumer-side strategies

StrategyTransient errorPoison pillOrderIn code
Stop & retry foreverRecovers, slowlypartition blocked; lag grows foreverKeptUncaught exception, seek() back to the failed offset
Log & skipLostLost (logged)Kepttry/catch around processing, then commit
Blocking retry → DLTRecovers after backoffTo DLT after N attemptsKeptSpring DefaultErrorHandler + BackOff + DeadLetterPublishingRecoverer
Retry topic → DLTRecovers in the retry topicTo DLT after N attemptsNot keptSpring @RetryableTopic, or your own *-retry topics
Production trap: blocking retries pause the whole partition, so their total time must stay well under max.poll.interval.ms, or the consumer is kicked out mid-retry and the group rebalances (Topic 2). Retry topics avoid the pause but give up per-key order. That's wrong for a subscriber's balance updates, but fine for independent CDRs.

Producer-side errors: retriable or not?

ExceptionTypeWhat the client does
NotLeaderOrFollowerExceptionretriableRefreshes metadata, retries on the new leader
NotEnoughReplicasExceptionretriableRetries until the ISR recovers or delivery.timeout.ms expires
TimeoutExceptionfinal after retriesReturned to your callback once delivery.timeout.ms runs out
RecordTooLargeExceptionnon-retriableFails immediately; raise max.request.size and the topic's max.message.bytes, or shrink the record
SerializationExceptionnon-retriableThrown by send() itself, before the record is buffered
TopicAuthorizationExceptionnon-retriableFix the ACLs (Module 7); retrying won't help
Admin takeaway: create the DLT with enough retention for someone to look at it, e.g. kafka-topics.sh --create --topic cdr.voice-dlt --partitions 6 --replication-factor 3 --config retention.ms=1209600000. Alert when it gets any new records, and replay fixed records from it back to cdr.voice. A DLT nobody reads just loses records more slowly.
