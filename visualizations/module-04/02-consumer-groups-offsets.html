Consumer groups, offset management & rebalancing

A consumer group splits a topic's partitions among its members. Each member reads only its own partitions and saves its progress as a committed offset in __consumer_offsets. Add consumers until the group keeps up, then crash one (click its box) and count how many CDRs get processed twice under each commit strategy.

Three offsets to keep apart

log-end offset
The offset the next produced record will get, per partition.
position
The next offset this consumer will fetch. It lives only in the consumer's memory.
committed offset
Stored in __consumer_offsets. A new owner starts reading here after a crash or rebalance.
consumer lag
log-end offset − committed offset. kafka-consumer-groups.sh --describe shows this per partition.

Auto commitenable.auto.commit=true, auto.commit.interval.ms=5000 (defaults); commits happen inside poll()Up to ~5 s of records reprocessedSimple consumers where duplicates are harmless

Kafka 4.x note: the new consumer rebalance protocol (KIP-848, group.protocol=consumer) is generally available. The group coordinator on the broker computes assignments and moves partitions one at a time, so there is no group-wide pause. The classic protocol (group.protocol=classic) is still the client default, and it is what this simulation shows.
Admin gotcha: a consumer that takes longer than max.poll.interval.ms (default 5 min) between poll() calls is kicked out of the group even though its heartbeats are fine. The group rebalances, its uncommitted work is replayed, and it can loop forever. Fix the processing time or lower max.poll.records. Adding consumers won't help. Check with kafka-consumer-groups.sh --bootstrap-server … --describe --group billing-svc.
